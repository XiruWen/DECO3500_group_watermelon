# Simulated Interview Transcript 1

**Participant:** P7 (de-identified)
**Interviewer:** Lingjie Ruan
**Background:** Hobbyist music producer who makes electronic music in their free time and occasionally uploads tracks online.

## Question 1

**Interviewer:**
Tell me about the last time someone you did not know discovered your music.

**Participant:**
A few months ago I uploaded a short track online. Normally only my friends listen to what I make, so I did not expect much.

A few days later, someone I did not know left a comment saying they liked the atmosphere of the track. I was quite surprised. It actually felt more meaningful than a comment from a friend because this person had no reason to support me.

**Interviewer:**
Did you want to know more about them?

**Participant:**
Not personal information, but I was curious about where they found the track and what they were doing when they listened to it. That context would make the reaction more interesting.

## Question 2

**Interviewer:**
Tell me about a place that influenced something you created.

**Participant:**
One evening I was walking near the river and had a melody in my head. I recorded it quickly on my phone.

When I got home, I turned it into a short track. The final track became quite calm, and I think the atmosphere of that evening influenced it.

**Interviewer:**
Do you still think about that place when you hear the track?

**Participant:**
Yes. The track is connected with that evening for me, even though other listeners would not know that.

## Question 3

**Interviewer:**
Tell me about a decision you made about where to share your music.

**Participant:**
I uploaded one track publicly because I thought it was good enough, but it did not get many listens.

That is quite normal when you only make music as a hobby. There is so much music online that uploading something does not mean anyone will actually find it.

**Interviewer:**
Does that affect how you share music now?

**Participant:**
Yes. Sometimes I prefer sharing music in a smaller context. If somebody finds it because of a place or situation, that feels more interesting than just being another song online.

## Question 4

**Interviewer:**
Tell me about a time someone's reaction to your music surprised you.

**Participant:**
I played an unfinished track to a friend. I thought the melody was the main part, but they noticed a small background sound.

They said it reminded them of travelling at night. I had never thought about the track that way.

**Interviewer:**
Did that change the track?

**Participant:**
A little. I kept that sound and made it slightly stronger. I like that different people can connect the same music with different memories.

## Question 5

**Interviewer:**
Tell me about a time you wanted to share your music but decided not to.

**Participant:**
That happens with unfinished tracks. Sometimes I want to send something to friends, but it can feel like I'm asking them to judge my work or give me attention.

**Interviewer:**
What would make sharing easier?

**Participant:**
If people could discover it naturally. Then they could choose whether they wanted to listen instead of me directly asking them.
