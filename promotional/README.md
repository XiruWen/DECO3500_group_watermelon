| [`EchoSpot_Manual_A4.pdf`](EchoSpot_Manual_A4.pdf) | Eight-page A4 landscape walkthrough, run on a tablet at the stall and used as a speaking aid |

The manual shows the prototype as it actually is: every screenshot was taken from the deployed
build at <https://xiruwen.github.io/DECO3500_group_watermelon/EchoSpot/>, on a phone-sized
viewport, with the device standing at UQ St Lucia. It covers what an echo is, the three steps
(leave, walk there, listen), answering, echoes that share a spot, and the smaller controls —
the search behaviour, Simulate location for indoor GPS, and the You page.
