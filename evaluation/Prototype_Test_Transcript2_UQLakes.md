### Questions after leaving the echo

**Facilitator:**  
Why this particular sound? Why this one rather than another one?

**S2:**  
Because it was already in my headphones when I lay down. I didn't plan it. It was just quiet and slow, and it matched the fact that I was trying to stop thinking for a few minutes. I could have chosen something more recognisable, but that would feel like choosing for other people. This was genuinely what I was listening to.

**Facilitator:**  
Why here? Would it still make sense if you left it somewhere else?

**S2:**  
It would make sense in another quiet park, probably. It isn't only for this exact patch of grass. But I wouldn't leave it inside the library or at a busy bus stop. Here you can lie down, look at the water and not do anything. The song fits that. So the exact coordinates aren't essential, but the kind of place is.

**Facilitator:**  
Who do you hope finds it? Is there anyone you'd rather didn't?

**S2:**  
Maybe another student who has a break between classes and is also tired. I don't mind who, really. I just wouldn't want someone to play it loudly on a speaker and annoy everyone. It feels more like a headphones thing.

**Facilitator:**  
What do you hope they do after listening—leave, reply with a song, or contact you?

**S2:**  
They can just listen and leave. I don't need a reply. If they reply with another calm song, that's fine, but I wouldn't want a message from them. That would make it feel more social than I intended.

**Facilitator:**  
If nobody ever responds, would you leave another one?

**S2:**  
Yes, because I wasn't really asking a question. If the app showed that one person listened, that would be enough. Even if it didn't, I might leave another one somewhere else if I happened to be in the right mood. I wouldn't do it every day.

**Facilitator:**  
Would you want your name on it, or not? Why?

**S2:**  
No real name. The penguin and the nickname are okay. I don't want someone from my course recognising that I was lying here instead of doing my assignment. That's not a serious concern, but I just don't see why my name is needed.

**Facilitator:**  
Was there anything you thought about leaving but decided not to? Why not?

**S2:**  
I thought about recording a short voice message saying I was overwhelmed and needed a break. Then I imagined a stranger hearing my voice, and it felt too personal. The instrumental track says roughly the same thing without me having to explain myself.
