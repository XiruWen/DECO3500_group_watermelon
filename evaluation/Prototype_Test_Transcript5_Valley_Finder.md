## Opening and consent

**Facilitator:**  
Thanks for helping with this prototype test. This is a university project about how people discover sounds that other people have left in everyday places. There are no right answers. Please say what you are thinking, including anything you dislike or do not understand. I would like to record the conversation, but your name will not appear anywhere. You can stop at any time. Is that okay?

**F2:**  
Yeah, sure.

## Finding task

**Facilitator:**  
You are outside Fortitude Valley station. Imagine this is your own phone. Open the prototype and explore anything that catches your attention. Please talk aloud while you do it.

**F2:**  
All right. Oh, there are quite a few dots around here. Are these recordings from people who were actually in these places?

**Facilitator:**  
They are sounds that other users left at those locations.

**F2:**  
So you're collecting Brisbane sounds? That's kind of cool. The nearest one has a microphone icon and says "Midnight in the Valley." It's forty-five metres away. That sounds like it could be a song or maybe someone performing outside the station.

**Facilitator:**  
Why did that one catch your attention?

**F2:**  
Mostly the title. It sounds like an actual track name. And the microphone makes me think it might be a singer or a busker. There are lots of musicians around the Valley, so I thought it might be something good that somebody recorded here.

**Facilitator:**  
What do you notice about the distance settings?

**F2:**  
It says one kilometre. That's fine. I don't need to make it bigger because there are already enough things around me. I probably only care about the closest few anyway.

**Facilitator:**  
Please open the echo.

**F2:**  
Okay, the nickname is "Some Passerby." That doesn't tell me much. I'll play it. Oh... it's just someone humming. Is that the whole thing?

**Facilitator:**  
Yes, that is the full recording. What are you thinking?

**F2:**  
Honestly, I'm not interested. I thought it would be a proper song, or at least a busker who could sing. It's just a person going "mm-mm-mm" for a few seconds. I don't know them, so I don't really care about hearing something they made up while they were standing here.

**Facilitator:**  
Did the location add anything to it?

**F2:**  
Not really. I can hear a bit of street noise, but it could have been recorded anywhere. The title made it sound more connected to the Valley than it actually was. Maybe if they explained why they were humming it—like they were nervous before going into a club or waiting for the last train—I might care more.

**Facilitator:**  
What did you expect before listening that was missing afterwards?

**F2:**  
I expected some quality, I guess. Not professional studio quality, but something worth discovering. The map and the title made it feel like there was a hidden song here. Then it was just a stranger humming with no context. That feels like opening a random voice message from someone I don't know.

**Facilitator:**  
Would you like it or respond to it?

**F2:**  
No. I closed it. I wouldn't leave a negative response because that's unnecessary, but I wouldn't tap the heart either. If the app asked me to reply, I'd probably skip it.

**Facilitator:**  
Would knowing the person's name make it more interesting?

**F2:**  
No. If it were a friend, maybe, but a stranger's name doesn't change the recording. A short note about why they left it would help more than their identity.

**Facilitator:**  
Would you explore another echo farther away after this?

**F2:**  
Probably not. There are other markers inside the one-kilometre circle, but this first one didn't give me much confidence. I'm not going to make the range larger and look for more. I might try one more nearby if the title clearly says it's a real track or a live performance.

**Facilitator:**  
If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**F2:**  
Tell me what kind of audio it is before I open it—released song, live performance, voice note, humming, ambient sound, whatever. I wouldn't have selected this if I knew it was just someone humming, but somebody else might be into that.

## Facilitator observation notes

- F2 initially reacted positively to the idea of collecting sounds around Brisbane.
- The title and 🎤 icon led F2 to expect a song, singer or busker performance.
- F2 showed visible disappointment after discovering that the echo was an unaccompanied humming recording.
- F2 closed the echo without liking it and did not want to respond.
- F2 noticed the 1.0 km radius but did not move the slider; the disappointing first result further reduced interest in distant echoes.
