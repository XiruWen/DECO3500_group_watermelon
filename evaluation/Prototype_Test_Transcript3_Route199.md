## Opening and consent

**Facilitator:**  
Thanks for helping with this test. This is a university project about how people share music in everyday places. There are no right answers. I am interested in what you actually do and what feels awkward or unclear. I would like to record the conversation, but your names will not appear anywhere. You can stop at any time. Is that okay?

**S3:**  
Yep, all good.

## Sharing task — S3 leaves an echo

**Facilitator:**  
You're waiting for the 199 bus. Please use the prototype to leave a sound here and talk aloud while you do it.

**S3:**  
Okay, I already know what I want. I want to leave Prince, *1999*, because it's the 199 bus. It's a bad joke, but that's why I like it. So I press plus... upload audio. Right, this is the problem. The song is in Spotify, not in my Files app. When I press upload, it only shows local files.

**Facilitator:**  
What would you normally expect to do here?

**S3:**  
Search for the song, or paste a link. I don't own an MP3 of it. I haven't had MP3s on my phone for years. There's a live recording option, but I can't play Spotify and record it on the same phone at the same time, can I?

**Facilitator:**  
For this test, you can play the track from your own phone and use the research phone to record a short excerpt.

**S3:**  
Okay, but that's pretty clumsy. Also the sound quality is going to be bad. Let me turn it up. There are buses going past as well. All right, I'll record... stop. You can hear the song, but you can also hear the crossing signal. Maybe that's actually more connected to the place.

**Facilitator:**  
What title will you use?

**S3:**  
"If you also take the 199." That tells people why the song is here. Otherwise it would just look like I randomly chose Prince. For the icon, headphones—🎧. I thought about the microphone, but I didn't sing it, and the fire icon feels too intense.

**Facilitator:**  
What do you think about the location information?

**S3:**  
Again, it's just coordinates. I know I'm at the right stop because I'm standing here, but I want it to say the bus stop name. The route number is the whole reason I'm leaving it. I'll press "Drop it here." Done.

### Questions after leaving the echo

**Facilitator:**  
Why this particular sound? Why this one rather than another one?

**S3:**  
Purely because of the number. Route 199, song *1999*. I heard the bus announcement and the song title came into my head. If this were the 60 bus, I wouldn't leave it. I know that's stupid, but it made me laugh while I was waiting.

**Facilitator:**  
Why here? Would it still make sense if you left it somewhere else?

**S3:**  
No, it needs to be somewhere the 199 actually stops. It could work at another stop on the same route, but not just anywhere in Queen Street. The location is basically the joke. If someone finds it without seeing a 199 bus, they might not get it.

**Facilitator:**  
Who do you hope finds it? Is there anyone you'd rather didn't?

**S3:**  
Someone who catches this bus regularly and notices the title. I don't mind tourists finding it, but they probably won't understand why it's here. I don't want someone playing it loudly at the stop because then everyone has to hear my joke. Headphones would be better. And, I guess, I wouldn't want anyone treating the audio like I uploaded the actual song legally, because I just recorded it off another phone.

**Facilitator:**  
What do you hope they do after listening—leave, reply with a song, or contact you?

**S3:**  
Reply with another route-related song or a better joke. Maybe someone who catches the 60 can find a song with 60 in the title. I don't want direct contact. I don't need a stranger messaging me about Prince.

**Facilitator:**  
If nobody ever responds, would you leave another one?

**S3:**  
Maybe, if another obvious idea comes up. But I wouldn't go looking for things to post. Also, the upload process was annoying enough that I probably wouldn't do this often. If I could just choose a song from a catalogue and attach it, then yes, I'd leave more.

**Facilitator:**  
Would you want your name on it, or not? Why?

**S3:**  
No. A nickname is better. It's just a small joke for another commuter. My real name adds nothing, and if the audio-sharing part is legally questionable, I definitely don't want my name attached.

**Facilitator:**  
Was there anything you thought about leaving but decided not to? Why not?

**S3:**  
I wanted to leave the actual track, cleanly, not this recording with traffic in it. I looked for it in the upload screen and then realised the app only accepts a file already stored on the phone. I decided not to download some random MP3 because that takes time and I don't know where it would come from. I also considered humming the chorus, but I wasn't going to do that at a crowded bus stop with people watching.

## Facilitator observation notes

- S3 first attempted **Upload audio** and could not access the streaming track as a local file.
- S3 used **Record live** only after the facilitator provided a second-device workaround.
- The workaround captured traffic and crossing noise, which reduced clarity but increased the sense of place.
- S3 requested a track link or catalogue reference rather than hosted copyrighted audio.
