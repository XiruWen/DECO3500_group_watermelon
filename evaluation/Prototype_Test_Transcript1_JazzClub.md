## Opening and consent

**Facilitator:**  
Thanks for helping with this test. This is a university project about how people share music in everyday places. There are no right answers. I am interested in what you actually do and what feels awkward or unclear. I would like to record the conversation, but your names will not appear anywhere. You can stop at any time. Is that okay?

**S1:**  
Yep, that's fine.

## Sharing task — S1 leaves an echo

**Facilitator:**  
Imagine the performance has just finished. Please use the prototype to leave one sound here. Talk aloud while you do it.

**S1:**  
Okay. So I press the plus button, right? Yeah. It opens on "Upload audio." I don't want that because the recording is from tonight, so I'll use "Record live." Hang on, I'll play the clip from my phone near this phone. Is that okay?

**Facilitator:**  
For this test, yes. Use the option that feels closest to what you would normally do.

**S1:**  
All right. I'll start it just before the saxophone bit. Wait... no, I missed it. Can I do it again?

**Facilitator:**  
Yes.

**S1:**  
Okay, second try. That's better. You can hear someone clapping at the end, but that's fine. Now it wants a title. Um... I'll call it "That solo by the river." It sounds a bit dramatic, but the club is literally beside the river, so it works. For the icon, I want a saxophone, but there isn't one. Piano is probably the closest. I'll choose the piano.

**Facilitator:**  
Which icon did you select?

**S1:**  
The piano, the little keyboard one—🎹. I nearly chose the microphone, but there wasn't really any singing in this part.

**Facilitator:**  
What do you understand from the location line at the bottom?

**S1:**  
It gives me latitude and longitude. I mean, I trust that it's here, but the numbers don't help me. I'd rather it said "Brisbane Jazz Club" or showed the street name. Anyway, I'll press "Drop it here." Okay, it says the echo has been left.

### Questions after leaving the echo

**Facilitator:**  
Why this particular sound? Why this section rather than another one?

**S1:**  
That was the part I kept thinking about after the set. They were playing *Autumn Leaves*, and the sax player kind of held one note, then the piano came back in underneath it. I don't know the technical words for it. It was just the point where I stopped talking and properly listened. There were louder parts and probably cleaner parts, but that was my favourite bit.

**Facilitator:**  
Why here? Would it still make sense if you left it somewhere else?

**S1:**  
Not as much. I could send the clip to a friend anywhere, obviously, but leaving it here feels like saying, "This happened in this room tonight." If I left it at a shopping centre, it would just be a random jazz clip. Here, someone might be arriving for another show or walking past the club and understand why it's here.

**Facilitator:**  
Who do you hope finds it? Is there anyone you'd rather didn't?

**S1:**  
Probably someone going to a gig here, or someone who likes live music but hasn't been inside before. I don't really care about their age or anything. I suppose I wouldn't want the musicians to find it and think I was uploading their work without asking. That part makes me a little uncomfortable. It was recorded on my phone, but it isn't my music.

**Facilitator:**  
What do you hope they do after listening—leave, reply with a song, or contact you?

**S1:**  
Reply with another little moment from a different night. That would be cool. I don't need them to contact me directly. Actually, I'd probably prefer they didn't. I like the idea of the place collecting different performances more than having a stranger message me.

**Facilitator:**  
If nobody ever responds, would you leave another one?

**S1:**  
Maybe once more. If I came back and heard something really good, yeah. But if I left two or three and there was never any reaction at all—not even a play count or a like—I would probably stop. I'd assume nobody was finding them.

**Facilitator:**  
Would you want your name on it, or not? Why?

**S1:**  
Not my real name. A nickname is enough. The sound is more about the night than about me, and, again, I didn't perform it. Putting my name next to it might make it look like I'm claiming something. "After Hours" feels safer and more in the mood of the place.

**Facilitator:**  
Was there anything you thought about leaving but decided not to? Why not?

**S1:**  
There was a longer section with the singer that sounded better as a complete song, but people at our table were talking over it. Also, it felt worse to upload a recognisable chunk of the performance. I picked the shorter instrumental bit because it felt more like a memory and less like copying the band's track. I still don't know whether the band would be okay with it, though.
