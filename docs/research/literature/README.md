# Literature critiques

## What the course actually asks for

From the **Week 2 (Domain work)** slides:

> "Critically review your chosen papers (**minimum of 2 each student**)"
>
> "A critical review does not mean you have to summarize or criticize the paper, it
> means you **evaluate its usefulness and value to your chosen problem space**.
> What can it tell you about designing for that domain?"
>
> "Be strategic — pick out **one or two** of the most relevant design implications or
> challenges from the paper and discuss them in relation to your interests."

From the **Week 3 (Team formation & research)** slides:

> "**Write for your audience: not just your tutors, but your future teammates.**"
>
> "Don't make me (or your teammates) have to read the paper ourselves to know what is
> useful/not useful about it for your domain — use your **~400 word critique** to tell
> us so they don't have to read it."
>
> "**Don't summarise whole papers.** Instead, pick specific aspects that have particular
> insights, or good application possibilities to your area."
>
> "Try to make **at least one paper connect to a social/mobile concept** relevant to your domain."

From the **Week 3 Annotated Portfolio Task**:

> Find two peer-reviewed papers **from the ACM Digital Library (dl.acm.org)** related to
> your team's chosen domain. Identify and highlight **at least two domain-relevant
> insights**. Annotate those insights by **articulating a design implication for each**.
> Identify at least **two Synchronous or Asynchronous + Co-located or Distributed
> opportunities** for social/mobile computing relevant to your domain.

> ⚠️ **The task names the ACM Digital Library specifically.** Sources from Nature, PLOS ONE
> and First Monday do not satisfy it. Search ACM venues: **CHI, CSCW, UbiComp, DIS, GROUP,
> OzCHI**, and the journals *TOCHI*, *JCSCW*, *Personal & Ubiquitous Computing*.

## Where to search

- **ACM Digital Library** — https://dl.acm.org (log in through the UQ Library to get past paywalls)
- **UQ Library search** and **Google Scholar** (not ordinary Google)

Suggested ACM DL queries for this domain:

```
"music sharing" AND mobile
"location-based" AND audio AND social
"place" AND "music" AND "serendipity"
"co-located" AND "music listening"
"asynchronous" AND "audio messages" AND place
"digital graffiti" OR "virtual graffiti" OR "location-based messaging"
"soundscape" AND "shared listening"
```

> The team's earlier source list included the MIT Media Lab **tunA** project (mobile,
> co-located music sharing) — find and cite its **published paper**, not the project page,
> and confirm the venue and year before citing.
