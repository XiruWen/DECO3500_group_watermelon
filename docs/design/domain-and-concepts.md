# Domain and concepts

## Domain statement

> We are interested in enhancing the way people discover music and connect with each other
> through what previous listeners leave behind at the same everyday places and activities
> (gyms, study spaces, commutes, live venues) — turning solitary listening into a shared
> experience — with the possibilities of social and/or contextual computing.

## Problem framing

Recommendation arrives the same way no matter where you are or who else is there.
**Place is the one dimension existing music products have never used to connect people to
each other** — where it is used at all, it is used to serve one listener better, never to
link the people who share a place.

The places we listen in every day — the gym, the study room, the bus stop — hold no trace
of the people who listened there before us.

## Concepts

### EchoNotes — traces left in a place
Leave a song tied to the spot you are standing in. The next person has to come here to hear
it. Traces are anonymous and are not all handed over at once: you pick them up by moving
around and lingering. Having picked one up, you can leave it be, or answer it with a track
of your own.

### EchoVibe — what people here are listening to right now
A shared queue for whoever is in this space. Each person can drop in one track; everyone
hears what the room is playing, through their own headphones. The room dissolves when
people leave.

### EchoTrail — a soundtrack that builds along a route
Songs left along a route someone travels every day — the same stop, the same walk. Over
weeks the route develops a soundtrack contributed by the people who share it.

## Course concepts this engages

| Concept | In EchoSpot |
|---|---|
| **Time–space matrix** | EchoNotes and EchoTrail sit in *different time / same place*; EchoVibe in *same time / same place* |
| **Awareness → Conversation → Mutual learning** | Picking up a trace gives awareness; answering it gives conversation; a place's chain accumulating over weeks gives mutual learning |
| **Contextual computing** | Context is not only coordinates — it is the atmosphere of a place and the moment someone chose to leave something there |
| **Social, not social media** | No profiles, no followers, no feed. The social unit is a **place**, not a person |

## Existing solutions

| Existing | Gap | How EchoSpot differs |
|---|---|---|
| **Spotify** | Discovery is algorithm- and playlist-based, with no connection to where the listener is or who else is there | Place-based, community-driven discovery — songs linked to specific real-world places |
| **Shazam** | Identifies what is playing around you, but the result is private and momentary; nothing is left behind for the next person | Traces persist in the place for whoever comes next |
| **Bandcamp** | You have to already know what you are looking for | You encounter things you were not looking for |
